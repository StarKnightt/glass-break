# Critique — System 3 (shooting + minimal UI) — FAIL

Shots: `shots/system3/critic/` (0-start, 1-default-hud, 2-zoomed, 3-zoom-after-wood-miss, 4-recoil-t0/t1/t2, 5-ground-miss-90ms, 6-closeup-ground-puff/after, 7-closeup-wood-hole, 7-closeup-wood-splinter-60ms, 8-after-reset, 9-after-esc, crop-xh-*, crop-hud). Close-ups use rig.baseFov=4 (telephoto from the eye).
Measured: vFOV 29°, bottle 60 px tall at 1600x900 / 6.7 m, recoil peak 0.49° pitch at 80 ms, still 0.23° at 200 ms, fire interval 250 ms, frame ~5 ms.

1. **Miss impacts read as CG, not bullet hits.** Wood "splinters" are round soft gaussian discs (point sprites) that look like bokeh/dust motes, are lighter than the wood and fly far (7-splinter-60ms). The hole is a ~11 mm soft dark dot like a sticker (7-wood-hole). Real hits on weathered grey wood show pale fresh wood: a light torn-fiber ring 2–3x the caliber around a dark 4.5–5.5 mm core, plus a few elongated slivers.
   Fix: draw splinters as stretched quads/thin boxes aligned to velocity with spin, pale yellow fresh-wood color (#c9a46a), 20–60 ms of fast travel then drop. Make the decal a 2-layer atlas (dark core 5 mm, irregular pale torn ring ~14 mm, slightly elongated along the grain) with a normal map. Add a faint fiber-dust puff that lasts 0.5 s.
2. **Ground miss is almost invisible.** At 300 ms the puff is a faint grey haze, darker and greyer than the orange dirt. There are no clods and no mark on the ground (6-closeup-*). Real dry dirt throws a sharp narrow spurt within 30 ms (0.2–0.4 m tall), then a soft cloud that drifts downwind for 1–2 s.
   Fix: two stages. Stage one is 6–10 opaque clods/grit, plus a vertical cone of dense sprites (alpha 0.7) rising at 3–5 m/s. Stage two is 4–6 large soft sprites (alpha 0.25) that grow 3x and drift with the wind. Sample the color from the ground albedo and brighten it 1.2x. Add a small dark crater decal (3–4 cm) with a lighter rim.
3. **Recoil is a slow, floaty nod.** It takes 80 ms to reach 0.49° and fades out smoothly over 300 ms or more. There is no position kick, no FOV punch (the research spec asks for −2.5° springing back over 150 ms) and no shake. Real airgun/rifle footage shows a sharp jolt within 10–20 ms, a little overshoot, then a fast settle with residual wobble.
   Fix: put the impulse into position (0.01 m back, 0.004 m up) and pitch (0.8–1.2° reached in about 15 ms). Use a stiffer spring with less damping (K≈600, ζ≈0.45) so it overshoots once. Add the FOV punch and 120 ms of trauma² perlin shake. Zoomed shots need a larger visible jump.
4. **Nothing says "person holding a rifle".** There is no barrel, no sight and no scope. Right-click is a plain 2x FOV lerp with the same game "+" crosshair (2-zoomed). At 2x the bottles are still only ~120 px tall.
   Fix: on zoom, show a scope view at 4–6x: a circular eyepiece with a hard black vignette, a thin duplex/mil-dot reticle, slight chromatic fringe, and an eye-relief "scope shadow" that shifts with sway. Alternatively, show a blurred barrel/front sight in the lower right when hip-aiming. Raise sway while zoomed (×1.5, breathing) instead of lowering it to 0.65.
5. **The pointer lock re-lock bug changes the control scheme.** After Esc, Chrome refuses `requestPointerLock` for about 1 s. `pointerlockerror` (and the 400 ms timeout) then sets `fallback=true` silently: the mouse switches to absolute-position aiming with a visible OS cursor. The 400 ms timer can also flip to fallback while a lock request is still pending.
   Fix: on a failed lock, keep the "click to resume" overlay up and retry, instead of falling back. Use fallback only when `'pointerLockElement' in document` is false. Hide the cursor (`cursor:none`) whenever fallback is active.
6. **Debug and hint UI break the "real footage" look.** The stats-gl panel (fps, calls, tris) is visible top-left without `?debug`, in every shot. The bottom hint is 10 px lowercase grey on grass, unreadable but still visible as clutter.
   Fix: gate stats behind `?debug` as the research spec says. Put the hint in the start overlay only, under "click to start", and drop it from the playfield.
7. **Hits get no feedback of their own.** A hit only bumps a 13 px counter. In fallback, a click inside the 250 ms cooldown is silently dropped, and nothing tells the player the shot did not fire.
   Fix: flash the crosshair briefly on hit (white to warm, 80 ms, no hitmarker X), and fire `onFire` sound/recoil only when a shot really fires. For airgun realism, raise `FIRE_INTERVAL` to 0.6–0.9 s with a subtle cocking cue. Then dropped clicks become believable.
8. **Small issues.**
   - The crosshair is 1 px at 78% white and disappears against the bright sky/rail glare. Add a 1 px dark outline (box-shadow 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.35)) and set the gap to 3 px.
   - Reset clears decals but not live particles. Zero `alpha[]` in `clearDecals`.
   - "Score" should be one number: show "hits / shots" as `3 / 5`, to stay within the score + reset brief.
   - Perf is fine: 5 ms/frame. The particle update still uploads all 384 entries every frame even when idle. Set `needsUpdate` only when `any` is true.
