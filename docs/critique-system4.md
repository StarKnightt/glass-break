# Critique — System 4 (fracture + shards) — VERDICT: FAIL
Shots: `shots/system4/critic/` — `{beer,soda,whiskey}_t{10,40,120,300,800}.jpg`, `*_settled_close.jpg`, `*_settled_ground.jpg`, `game_intact.jpg`, `game_burst_t{40,150}.jpg`, `game_settled.jpg`. Capture script: `C:\tmp\shot\critic4.mjs` (hit at 40% height, facing the camera, time scale 0.05).
Stats: beer 87–92 shards, soda 163, whiskey 262 (no reset between bottles, so these are running totals; about 90–100 new each). 130 glitter per break. Background fracture worker takes 25–54 ms. Five-bottle burst in the game view: median frame 5.0 ms, p90 5.5, max 8.4, 277 shards moving at once.

1. **Game camera can't see the break.** `game_burst_t150` shows a green puff about 30 px wide, and `game_settled` shows no shards on the rail or the ground. The refs fill the frame with a dense cloud. Fix: slow-mo should punch the camera in toward the hit bottle (that's system 7, but fracture depends on it). Scale burst spread and glitter brightness to how big the bottle is on screen. Keep at least 30% of the shards on or in front of the rail, and bias the burst toward the camera.
2. **Shards are opaque, flat-shaded confetti, not glass.** Beer shards are solid saturated green cards. Soda shards are milky white. Whiskey shards are pink/red, which looks like label or cap colour on every piece; whiskey glass is clear. The refs (cavitation, coke) show nearly clear, refractive plates that are dark except where bright edges and sun glints catch. Fix: use the bottle's own transmission material on the faces, or a cheap version: alpha around 0.25–0.4, a Fresnel term, a sun specular highlight, and a bright greenish edge on the fracture side faces. Only label-backed pieces should be opaque paper.
3. **Crack phase is a fake spider-web decal.** `beer_t40` shows evenly spaced white 1 px rings and spokes over the whole intact bottle, with nothing moving yet (0 shards awake at 40 ms on every bottle). In the coke and cavitation refs the bottle is already bulging and crumbling at the hit point. Cracks are few, uneven and refractive, not chalk lines. Fix: shorten the crack phase to about 5–15 ms of game time. Make cracks 2–3 px with a bright core and a falloff, only a few irregular branches, dense at the hit point and stopping partway. Start breaking pieces off the impact zone during the crack phase.
4. **Shard sizes are too even, and there's no powder.** At 120 ms the pieces are all around 1–2 cm and look like cut triangles. The coke ref shows a white halo of glass dust plus fine needles around the hit, then a few large curved plates. Fix: use a power-law size distribution: about 70% of shards under 5 mm (glitter or instanced slivers) clustered within 3 cm of the hit, and 3–6 large curved plates over 4 cm wide. Add a short-lived bright glass-dust cloud (about 150 ms) at the hit point.
5. **Shards don't look curved or sharp.** They read as flat polygons with no thickness. The refs show plates that keep the bottle's curvature, plus long slivers. Fix: keep the lathe curvature on each shard, give the edges a 2–3 mm thickness with bright side faces, and make 15–20% of shards long slivers (aspect ratio over 4).
6. **The neck flies straight up, upright and intact, as if launched.** In `beer_t120` and `whiskey_t120` it rises vertically above the cloud. In real footage the neck usually stays roughly in place, tips over, or drops with only small upward motion. Fix: give the neck mostly the impulse along the bullet's path plus a small random tumble. Limit its upward speed to under 1 m/s and add strong spin about a horizontal axis.
7. **Whiskey base ring glows like a lamp** (`whiskey_t120`, `whiskey_settled_close`). It's a large bright emissive slab, which is an obvious CG tell. The `broken_brown_bottle_base_survives` ref shows a dark, jagged-edged base. Fix: make the base use the regular glass material with a jagged top edge, and clamp the bloom from its specular highlight.
8. **Burst direction and spread.** The cloud is a symmetric vertical spray over the rail. No exit-side jet away from the shooter is visible, and many pieces go behind the fence (`soda_t300`). Fix: exit side about 3× stronger in a cone of about 35°. Entry-side spall should be small and fast toward the camera. Cap the backward spread so most shards land within 1.5 m.
9. **Few settled shards remain, and resting is unreadable.** `beer_settled_ground` shows 3–4 specks on the rail and almost nothing on the dirt. The refs show a glittering scatter that lasts. Fix: make the ground shards larger and flat, with a sun-glint flicker material. Keep at least 40 visible rest pieces on the rail and the dirt in front of it. Snap resting orientation flat, which is still not convincing.
10. **Perf and timing.** The 25–54 ms fracture worker time explains the dead 40 ms with nothing moving. There's no cap on moving shards: 277 were awake after five fast hits. Frame time is fine now but will grow without a limit. Fix: precompute fracture templates per bottle type when the scene loads so shatter is instant on the hit frame. Cap moving shards at about 300 and put the oldest to sleep when over.

Spin rates look fine in the frames but can't be measured from stills; small pieces should still spin noticeably faster than large ones.
